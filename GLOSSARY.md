# TableTennisAz

Terms for the players, ratings, and match results published on tabletennis.az.

## Language

**Player**:
A table-tennis participant listed on the site with a public profile and playing record.

**Amateur player**:
A player listed in the website's Amateur category, including a player also listed in its Professional category.

**Player rating**:
A player's points total in the site's rating system. A rating differs from the player's position in an ordered list.

**Amateur rank**:
A player's position among amateur players, ordered by rating points from highest to lowest. Equal points share a rank and skip the occupied positions before the next rank (1, 2, 2, 4); only amateur players occupy positions in this ranking.

**Source rank**:
A player's position within their category on the website.

**Player profile**:
A player's public information, including their rating and match history.

**Rating history**:
A player's or doubles pair's rating-point values over time. For doubles pairs, it records the first observed points and each later change.

**Ranking history**:
A player's or doubles pair's positions in a specified ranking over time. For doubles pairs, it records the first observed position and each later change.

**Match result**:
The recorded outcome of a completed match, identifying its participants and final score.

**Event**:
A competition under which the website groups matches and their results.

**Completed tournament**:
A tournament listed among the website’s finished competitions with published match results.

**Singles tournament**:
A tournament in which individual players compete for the tournament standings.

**Doubles tournament**:
A tournament in which doubles pairs compete for the tournament standings.

**Team tournament**:
A tournament in which team lineups compete for the tournament standings. A fixture between teams may include singles or doubles matches.

**New tournament**:
A completed singles or doubles tournament first listed in the website’s Results index after the index snapshot used for the device’s initial successful tournament refresh. Once it appears in its category, it stays new until the user opens that list.

**Tournament results publication**:
The first time a completed singles or doubles tournament appears in the app with its match results available to open.

**Tournament podium**:
The medal placements and their finishers published by the website for a completed tournament.

**Tournament stage**:
The part of a tournament in which a match is played, such as group play, a knockout round, or the third-place match.

**Group match**:
A match between entrants assigned to the same tournament group.

**Duel**:
A match published in the website's separate duels section. Duels can be singles or doubles.

**Results feed**:
A list of completed event-match and duel results, ordered by match date from newest to oldest. It can be narrowed to event matches, duels, or a selected event.

**Singles match**:
A match between two individual players, with one player on each side.

**Doubles match**:
A match between two pairs, with two players competing together on each side.

**Doubles pair**:
A partnership of two players competing together in doubles, regardless of the order of the teammates. A player can belong to several distinct pairs by partnering with different teammates.

**Head-to-head (Üz-üzə)**:
The match history between two opposing players in singles or two opposing doubles pairs.

**Head-to-head score**:
The number of match wins for each side across their completed head-to-head matches.

**Doubles tournament pair count**:
The tournament’s published total of doubles pairs, or the number of distinct pairs in its complete published match results when the website provides only an individual player count.

**Ranking movement**:
The change in a doubles pair's published position between two observations of the doubles ranking. A lower position number is an upward movement; a higher number is a downward movement.

**Doubles profile**:
A doubles pair's public information, including its match results, medal counts, rating history, and ranking history.

**Doubles ranking**:
The website's ordered list of doubles pairs, using its published pair positions and points. Each partnership is ranked separately; pairs with equal points can occupy different published positions.

**Doubles ranking observation**:
The published points and positions seen for doubles pairs when the doubles ranking was checked.

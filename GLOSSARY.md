# TableTennisAz

Terms for the players, ratings, and match results published on tabletennis.az.

## Language

**Player**:
A table-tennis participant listed on the site with a public profile and playing record.

**Amateur player**:
A player listed in the website's Amateur category, including a player also listed in its Professional category.

**Player rating**:
A player's points total in the site's rating system. A rating differs from the player's position in an ordered list.

**Amateur rank**:
A player's position among amateur players, ordered by rating points from highest to lowest. Equal points share a rank and skip the occupied positions before the next rank (1, 2, 2, 4); only amateur players occupy positions in this ranking.

**Source rank**:
A player's position within their category on the website.

**Player profile**:
A player's public information, including their rating and match history.

**Rating history**:
A player's or doubles pair's rating-point values over time. It is distinct from ranking history.

**Ranking history**:
A player's or doubles pair's positions in a specified ranking over time. It is distinct from rating history.

**Match result**:
The recorded outcome of a completed match, identifying its participants and final score.

**Event**:
A competition under which the website groups matches and their results.

**Duel**:
A match published in the website's separate duels section. Duels can be singles or doubles.

**Results feed**:
A list of completed event-match and duel results, ordered by match date from newest to oldest. It can be narrowed to event matches, duels, or a selected event.

**Singles match**:
A match between two individual players, with one player on each side.

**Doubles match**:
A match between two pairs, with two players competing together on each side.

**Doubles pair**:
A partnership of two players competing together in doubles. Partnerships with different teammates are separate pairs, so a player can belong to more than one pair.

**Ranking movement**:
The change in a doubles pair's published position between two observations of the doubles ranking. A lower position number is an upward movement; a higher number is a downward movement.

**Doubles profile**:
A doubles pair's public information, including its match results, medal counts, rating history, and ranking history.

**Doubles ranking**:
The website's ordered list of doubles pairs, using its published pair positions and points. Each partnership is ranked separately; pairs with equal points can occupy different published positions.

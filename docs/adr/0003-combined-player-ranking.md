# Rank amateur and professional players together

The original singles scope below is superseded by the revised singles scope
recorded in this document and [issue #14](https://github.com/eshref/TableTennisAz/issues/14).

## Original singles scope

The website publishes separate amateur and professional rankings, each starting
at 1. The app will merge both groups into one list without a category filter,
ordered by source rating points from highest to lowest, and display names,
points, and app-defined combined ranks. This provides the single list requested
by the project owner; an official combined ranking and cross-category point
comparability have not been established from the published website material.

Equal points receive equal ranks using competition ranking: 1, 2, 2, 4. Preserve
the distinction between the app's combined ranks and the website's source ranks.

## Revised singles scope

The singles ranking will include only amateur players, with no option to show
professional players. This replaces the combined-player inclusion decision above.
Positions will be recalculated among amateurs by rating points, retaining
competition ranking for ties (1, 2, 2, 4). Players listed only in the Professional
category will not occupy positions in the amateur ranking.
This exclusion applies only to the singles ranking. Professional players remain
accessible through player profiles, match results, and doubles rankings.
The website's Amateur list determines eligibility. Every player on that list is
included, even if the website also lists the player in its Professional category.

Previously saved mixed rankings do not record player categories and will not be
shown after the update. Until an amateur ranking is downloaded, the app will show
a message that a download is needed. Once downloaded, the amateur ranking will
remain available offline.

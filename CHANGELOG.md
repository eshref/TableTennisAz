# Changelog

## Unreleased

## 0.6.1 - 2026-11-21

- Singles and doubles tournament cards show dates in Azerbaijani and use icons
  for the date, entrant count, and prize. The layout wraps these details on
  smaller screens, and the podium shows framed portraits and bolder names.
- Qoşa turnirlər and Duellər have distinct icons in the app menu.
- The privacy policy lists the new support address,
  `tabletennisaz.support@gmail.com`.

## 0.6.0 - 2026-11-21

- Search completed doubles tournaments in Qoşa turnirlər, see pair counts where
  available, and open a tournament's match results.
- See published medalists on the podium for singles and doubles tournaments.
- Spot new tournaments by the red dots in the menu. Opening a tournament list
  clears its own dot.
- Open Reytinq, Şəxsi turnirlər, Qoşa turnirlər, and Duellər directly from the
  menu.

## 0.5.0 - 2026-11-21

- Search completed singles tournaments from the Şəxsi turnirlər menu. Open a
  tournament to browse its match results. Previously loaded tournaments and
  matches are available offline.
- Open Duellər from the app menu to browse singles and doubles duels together.
- Results cards show the tournament stage beside the tournament name when
  the website publishes a recognized stage. Saved results keep their stages offline.
- Open the TableTennisAz privacy policy from the app menu.

## 0.4.0 - 2026-11-18

First officially signed Android APK, available from GitHub Releases.

If you installed a phone-review debug APK, uninstall it once before installing
this release. Uninstalling clears saved app data, including offline rankings,
profiles, and results.

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-18

Android debug APK baseline for phone review.

- Browse and search the amateur player and doubles rankings. Open a player or
  doubles profile from its ranking row.
- View player rating history and doubles rating and ranking histories. Select a
  chart observation to see its date and points or position.
- Browse the Results feed by day or filter it to tournament matches, duels, or
  a selected event. Open a match to see the score, winner, and links to the
  participants’ profiles.
- Return to your searches and browsing position when switching between Rankings
  and Results. Revisit saved rankings, profiles, and results offline.
